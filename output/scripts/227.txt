The wooden follower of my late husband Harlan's Dutch cheese press was off balance by exactly eleven ounces. My name is not important. You do not inherit it, and you cannot transfuse it into someone who lacks it. You can only set boundaries and let the clotting do its work. Some people think I should have felt sorry for Brielle. I do not. I felt sorry for the little girl she used to be, the one who used to help me feed the goats and who laughed when the kids tried to jump on her back. But that little girl grew up and tried to bury me alive, and you do not owe your future to someone who practices your obituary. You owe it to yourself to stand up, to keep the original, to let the notary stamp glow under the light.
